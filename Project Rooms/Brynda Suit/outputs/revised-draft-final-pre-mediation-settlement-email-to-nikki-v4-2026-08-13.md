I am writing to give you one final opportunity to resolve this case before mediation and before everyone incurs still more expense. I am being direct because this dispute could have been addressed without a lawsuit and can still be brought to a final end now.

For settlement discussion, we preliminarily estimate the present value of the initial five-year housing benefit at approximately **$82,000**. That estimate assumes monthly market rent of $1,700, a $150 monthly tenant cost, and a 5% annual discount rate. We believe this lawsuit was unnecessary and that its allegations do not match what happened. Brynda contacted me while facing foreclosure. She said she had no one to help her and nowhere to go if the property was lost. The arrangement addressed both problems: it stopped the foreclosure and gave her the right to remain in the home indefinitely at an annual rent tied to the property-insurance cost. She accepted that arrangement and received its central benefit.

Brynda did not act without alternatives. Before contacting me, she met with another prospective buyer and took that buyer to Attorney Huston to discuss a proposed sale. She also testified that she was receiving many purchase inquiries because of the foreclosure and selected my communication because she considered it the most professional and preferred someone local. The exact amount of the other buyer's proposal is disputed in the record, but the important fact is not: she understood that investors wanted to purchase the property, consulted her own attorney, considered alternatives, and prioritized being able to remain in the home.

After the transaction, we treated Brynda with the same care and concern we would have shown our own mother. I drove her to medical appointments and provided a system for ordering groceries for delivery. Brynda herself testified that I was her emergency contact, was about the only person she saw outside county workers or the mail carrier, ordered groceries, took her to the bank and medical appointments, and responded when she needed help. We did not demand compensation for that assistance.

The foreclosure history raises a serious credibility issue. After Brynda met with the other prospective buyer and Attorney Huston, Huston contacted you and documented his understanding that you were willing to help Brynda financially. The record later shows that I cured the immediate arrears with a payment of $6,266.78. The opportunity to provide financial help existed before I became involved. Had that assistance been provided, the foreclosure potentially could have been cured without any transaction with me.

Your deposition testimony also presents issues that will have to be addressed if this case continues. You testified that you first learned about the foreclosure from Frances in 2024. That account is difficult to reconcile with Huston's September 2023 contact and contemporaneous email. Huston could not recall the exact words used during the call, so I am not overstating what he said; the timing and surrounding record nevertheless remain significant. You also admitted using Brynda's phone to communicate with me as though you were Brynda, allowing me to believe the messages came from her, and withholding your identity. The will predates the petition, gives the residue of Brynda's estate to you outright, and names you as personal representative. The healthcare power of attorney also predates the petition and documents that you held a significant fiduciary role concerning Brynda's healthcare. The GAL appointment was later vacated after our counsel challenged the lack of notice, hearing, and an incompetency finding.

That document sequence raises substantial questions about the accuracy and completeness of the disinterested-party representation, your direct financial interest in the litigation, and the factual basis on which the petition and related pleadings were filed. If this case continues, we intend to ask our counsel to evaluate every civil remedy supported by the record, including Rule 11 sanctions, recovery of fees and costs caused by any improper filing, and any separate civil claims that become legally available after a favorable outcome. We will also ask counsel to evaluate the responsibility of every person who signed, filed, or relied upon the petition. It does place you on notice that the documents are in hand and that we intend to preserve the civil issues they present. **[KEITH MUST CONFIRM THE PETITION LANGUAGE, DOCUMENT CHRONOLOGY, AND PERMISSIBLE REMEDIES BEFORE EXTERNAL USE.]**

I had known Brynda for only about two days and had approximately an hour and a half of conversation with her before the transaction. She contacted me, had consulted her own attorney about another proposal, was receiving other purchase inquiries, and had multiple options. I offered a solution that allowed her to avoid foreclosure and remain in her home; I did not control or overbear her decision.

After the property is renovated and sold, we will pay **100% of the positive net sale proceeds, if any**, calculated from the gross sale price after deducting the mortgage payoff, valid liens against this property, rehabilitation expenses, real-estate commission, closing costs, and documented taxes, insurance, utilities, and carrying costs through closing.
