Exhibit D  Summary of Contradictions in Natalie Dohertys Testimony
This exhibit highlights key contradictions between the deposition testimony of Natalie Doherty and
either (1) Brynda Griffiths deposition, or (2) the factual record of Bryndas documented statements
and conduct.
1. Natalie Claims Brynda Was Afraid of Wes
   - Natalie stated that her mother was intimidated or afraid of Wes Browning.
   - Contradiction: Brynda repeatedly rode alone with Wes, asked him to take her to appointments,
and even called Wes from the hospital in August 2023 asking him to bring her home. These actions
demonstrate trust and contradict the claim of fear.
2. Natalie Implies Brynda Lacked Mental Capacity
   - Natalie portrayed Brynda as impaired and not fully understanding her decisions in 2023.
   - Contradiction: In 2024, Brynda executed a valid Last Will and Testament. She was also coherent
in written communications, grocery lists, and conversations with Wes, including asking whether
anything would be left in her estate and commenting positively about the sale.

   - Natalie suggested Brynda was unaware of the nature and consequences of the real estate
