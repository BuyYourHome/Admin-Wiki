## Preliminary Filing Direction

The retained revocation rights and lifetime benefit provisions support grantor-trust treatment, assuming unchanged terms and circumstances throughout 2025. Income/deductions would generally be attributable to the grantor owners. A separate federal Form 1041 may be avoidable under an eligible optional reporting method, but prior reporting method, payer TINs, ownership and applicable exclusions must be checked before marking it unnecessary. Joint-filing spouses treated as owners of the whole trust are treated as one grantor for these optional reporting methods; do not generalize that special reporting rule into an automatic LLC-classification conclusion.
