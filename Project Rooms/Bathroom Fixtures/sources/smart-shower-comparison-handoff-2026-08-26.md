# Smart Shower Comparison Handoff

Date discussed: 2026-08-26

## Request

Wes asked for a comparison of Moen and Kohler smart shower systems.

## Working Conclusion

- Best two-outlet value: Kohler Anthem Duo.
- Best documented smart-home compatibility: Moen Smart Shower.
- Best multi-outlet and custom-shower platform: Kohler Anthem.
- Overall preliminary recommendation for a major remodel: Kohler Anthem, unless Apple HomeKit or broad voice-assistant support is a deciding requirement.

This is a preliminary product comparison, not a purchase or installation authorization.

## Comparison Summary

| Feature | Moen Smart Shower | Kohler Anthem |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outlet configurations | 2 or 4 | 2, 3, 4, or 6; Anthem+ expands further |
| Temperature control | Yes | Yes |
| Electronic flow-volume control | No | Yes |
| Main control | Tactile buttons and non-touch display | Touchscreen and rotary dial |
| Presets | 12 in app; 2 on controller | 9 on Anthem; 8 on Anthem Duo |
| Remote operation | App and documented Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit/Siri support | Kohler Konnect app and optional remote on/off button; verify exact-model voice support |
| Water-use tracking | No | Yes on supported Anthem configurations |
| Battery backup | Optional | No comparable backup identified in the reviewed materials |
| Multiple physical controls | One Moen controller per valve | Up to two Anthem controls on a supported system |

## Installation Considerations

Both product families generally require:

- an accessible concealed digital valve;
- a nearby 120V GFCI receptacle;
- a controller-to-valve cable;
- adequate hot-water capacity and incoming pressure;
- service access after finish work; and
- professional plumbing and electrical review.

Do not permanently enclose the digital valve without an approved service-access plan.
