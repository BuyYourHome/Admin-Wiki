# Missing Context

## Smart Shower Decision

- Property and bathroom location.
- Remodel stage and whether walls are open.
- Desired number and type of shower outlets.
- Desired finish.
- Equipment-only and installed budgets.
- Available hot-water capacity and incoming pressure.
- Accessible valve location and access-panel plan.
- Nearby GFCI receptacle and electrical scope.
- Smart-home platform and importance of voice, app, or HomeKit control.
- Preference for tactile controls versus touchscreen controls.
- Installer or plumber requirements and preferred service brand.
