## Residential exam and test day

- **Format:** PSI's Residential Contractor outline lists **90 scored questions**, **210 minutes**, and **70% to pass** (63 of 90). The exam is open book. PSI may add **5–10 unscored experimental questions**; it says time spent on those does not count against examination time. The computer tutorial can take up to **15 minutes outside exam time**. Its sample questions appear **at the test center after the tutorial**, not in the downloaded bulletin. Source: [PSI bulletin, “Tutorial,” “Experimental Questions,” and “Residential Contractor,” printed pp. 4, 24](https://test-takers.psiexams.com/api/content/bulletin/447).
- **Allowed references for this exam:** NC Occupational Safety and Health Standards for the Construction Industry (latest amendments); *Contractor’s Guide to Business, Law and Project Management — North Carolina General Contractors Edition*, 10th ed.; *NC State Building Code: Residential Code*, 2018; and *NC State Building Code: Administrative Code and Policies*, 2018. PSI lists the Board's [2026 Laws and Regulations book](https://www.nclbgc.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2026-Laws-And-Regulations-Book-DEC-3-2025_final-1.pdf) as a **study source that is not permitted in the Residential exam room**. Use PSI's current allowed/not-allowed lists before acquiring or preparing books. Source: [PSI bulletin, “Residential Contractor — Reference List,” printed pp. 24–25](https://test-takers.psiexams.com/api/content/bulletin/447).
- **Entry and materials:** Bring one valid, unexpired, signature-bearing government photo ID matching the registration name. A calculator must be silent, battery operated, nonprogrammable, nonprinting, and without an alphabet keyboard. PSI requires approved references to be complete and bound; highlighting, underlining, and permanent tabs may be added before the exam, but no writing during it, removable tabs, notes on tabs, or loose/attached extra papers. Electronics and other personal belongings go in secure storage, not the exam room. Source: [PSI bulletin, “Required Identification,” “Security Procedures,” and Residential reference rules, printed pp. 3, 24–25](https://test-takers.psiexams.com/api/content/bulletin/447).

## License and exam sequence

The Board distinguishes **classification** (type of work, such as Residential) from **limitation** (project size: limited, intermediate, or unlimited). Passing a qualifier exam alone does not issue a contractor license. For a new license, use the Board's [current online application entry point](https://nclbgc.org/license-applicants/), not an older paper application PDF. The [Board FAQ](https://nclbgc.org/faq-contractors/) says to complete the new-license application before exam prequalification. After review, the Board emails an eligibility letter; the candidate then schedules and pays for the exam with [PSI](https://test-takers.psiexams.com/nccon). PSI says its eligibility letter allows one examination within **120 days**. After the required exam is passed, the Board reviews the application for licensure. Sources: [Board applicant page](https://nclbgc.org/license-applicants/), [Board FAQ, “New License”](https://nclbgc.org/faq-contractors/), [PSI bulletin, “Guidelines” and “Registration,” printed p. 1](https://test-takers.psiexams.com/api/content/bulletin/447).
