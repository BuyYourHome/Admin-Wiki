# Missing Context

These are discovery questions, not blockers to creating the room:

- Which house/project and which rooms or lighting zones?
- Desired uses, appearance, brightness, color temperature, color rendering, and any color-changing requirements?
- Drawings, measured run lengths, quantities, mounting locations, and wet/damp or other environmental conditions?
- Existing fixtures, switches, dimmers, supplies, wiring, circuits, and exact model numbers?
- Desired wall controls, dimming method, smart-home integration, and offline/manual behavior?
- Accessible supply locations, cable routes, serviceability, and electrician constraints?
- Budget, purchasing timeline, preferred vendors, and responsible installer?
- Approved external source/output folder for drawings, photos, manufacturer PDFs, and spreadsheets?

Do not infer answers or select components until the relevant inputs and compatibility evidence exist.
