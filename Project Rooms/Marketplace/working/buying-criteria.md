# Marketplace Buying Criteria

## Current Starter Rule

- Minimum net profit margin: 25%.
- Margin formula: estimated net profit divided by conservative expected resale proceeds.
- Estimated net profit: expected resale proceeds minus purchase price, travel/pickup cost, selling fees, shipping, repairs, cleaning, missing parts, and other expected deal costs.
- Maximum purchase price: 75% of conservative expected resale proceeds minus all expected non-purchase costs.
- If the resale estimate or expected costs are not reliable enough to support the calculation, do not send an offer.
